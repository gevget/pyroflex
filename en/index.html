Thermal protection coating

Therma Ultra

The thermal protection layer in PYROFLEX ® Ultra systems helps the system maintain its specified fire resistance rating during a fire.

Certificates

Therma Epoxy

The thermal protection layer in PYROFLEX ® Epoxy systems is designed for demanding service conditions and the required fire resistance rating.

Certificates

PX-SYS-T

PYROFLEX ® System-T

A structural passive fire protection system for steelwork with a required thermal protection layer. System-T is an engineered solution in which the thermal layer forms and stabilizes the fire protection envelope during a fire.

System-T Ultra

A polyurethane-based structural fire protection system. It combines a thermal insulation layer with an intumescent fire protection layer.

Certificates

System-T Epoxy

An epoxy-based structural fire protection system for steel structures, consisting of thermal insulation and fire protection compounds.

Certificates
